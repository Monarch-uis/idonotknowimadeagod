# Initial Concept
An automated pipeline for converting EPUB books into high-quality audiobooks and videos, featuring multi-speaker support, background music mixing, and a comprehensive web-based GUI.

# Product Vision: EPUB to Audiobook/Video Converter

## Overview
This project transforms digital literature into immersive multimedia experiences. By combining advanced TTS synthesis with precise subtitle timing and dynamic video effects, it enables creators and readers to generate high-quality content autonomously.

## Target Audience
- **Content Creators:** YouTubers and influencers automating "book-to-video" production with a focus on high production value (multi-speaker, BGM).
- **Avid Readers:** Users seeking a premium, accessible listening experience with visual aids.

## Core Goals
1. **End-to-End Automation:** Seamless conversion from text to final video with minimal manual intervention.
2. **Immersive Storytelling:** Support for multi-speaker roles and atmospheric background music to bring books to life.
3. **Rock-Solid Reliability:** Resilient processing with auto-recovery for massive projects.
4. **Accessible Management:** A powerful, web-based interface for configuring, previewing, and monitoring conversions.

## Key Features
- **Multi-Speaker TTS:** Intelligent role assignment for diverse character voices using Piper and Edge-TTS.
- **Background Music Integration:** Automated selection and mixing of atmospheric tracks.
- **Visuals:** Optimized subtitle rendering, simple blur effects for backgrounds, and support for custom user thumbnails.
- **Full-Featured GUI:** A comprehensive web-based and application interface for project management and voice previews.
- **Robust Backend:** Checkpoint-based processing and detailed performance profiling.
