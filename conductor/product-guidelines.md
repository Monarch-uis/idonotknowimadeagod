# Product Guidelines

## 1. Design Philosophy
**"Brutalist Precision"**
The interface must balance raw, technical power with a sophisticated, high-impact visual presentation. It is a tool for creators who value efficiency and aesthetics equally.

## 2. Visual Identity
- **Theme:** Ultra-modern, monochromatic (Black & White) with a single bold accent color (Blue).
- **Layout:** Asymmetrical grids, breaking standard bootstrap constraints.
- **Typography:** Bold, oversized typography for headers and hero sections. High-contrast sans-serif for technical data.
- **Key Elements:**
    - Full-screen immersive hero sections with video background capabilities.
    - Visible, interactive hover effects (micro-interactions).
    - "Artstation-style" polish—clean but structurally bold.

## 3. User Experience (UX) Priorities
- **Efficiency First:** Dense information density where necessary. Keyboard shortcuts and logs should be easily accessible.
- **Visual Feedback:** The UI must communicate processing state clearly (e.g., real-time waveforms, video thumbnails).
- **Error Handling:** Transparent and technical. Display full stack traces and logs to allow for rapid debugging.

## 4. Content Presentation
- **Subtitles & Text:** Flexible rendering supporting three modes:
    1. **Professional:** Clean, minimal, utilitarian.
    2. **Story-Focused:** Serif/Genre-specific fonts to match the book's tone.
    3. **High-Visibility:** Large, bold text with high-contrast backdrops for readability.
- **Video Output:** Focus on "Simple Blur" background effects to maintain focus on the text without over-animating (no complex Ken Burns).
